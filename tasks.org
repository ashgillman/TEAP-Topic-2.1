## Core distinction

Do not portray this as a single strict legislative chain. Clearly distinguish:

1. Scientific evidence
2. International recommendations
3. International safety requirements and guidance
4. Australian fundamentals, codes, standards and guidance
5. Queensland Acts, Regulations and statutory standards
6. Licence and approval conditions
7. The approved local RSPP
8. Departmental procedures and work instructions
9. Records demonstrating implementation

Label each document or relationship by its actual authority:

- legally binding legislation
- subordinate legislation
- statutory radiation-safety standard
- enforceable licence or approval condition
- approved local plan
- national code capable of jurisdictional adoption
- non-mandatory guidance or recommendation
- technical evidence or professional consensus

Do not call a recommendation or guideline “law” unless it has acquired legal force through adoption, incorporation, a licence condition or another identified mechanism.
