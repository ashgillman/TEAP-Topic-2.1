* Authority and jurisdiction
- International technical guidance for States and competent authorities on transport security. It is not a Queensland licence condition by itself.
- It is later than RPS 11 (2019), so it is relevant international context rather than an asserted historical source for that code.

* Key takeaways :AI:
- Transport security is graded by material category and differs from transport radiation-safety package controls.
- Responsibilities, security planning and continuity of custody matter through preparation, carriage and receipt.
- Operational route, equipment and response details are intentionally omitted here.

* Summary :AI:
** Sections 1–3 — Scope, categorisation and regulation
- Explains transport-security levels, regulator responsibilities and the safety-security interface.
** Sections 4–6 — Systems, measures and plans
- Describes design, implementation and review of graded transport-security arrangements.
** Section 7 — Maintaining security during transport
- Addresses continuity of responsibility and custody through transport and handover.

* Practical relationship to the THHS RSPP
- Gives international context for HDR source transport security. Queensland Regulation Part 5 Division 3 and prescribed Australian security and transport codes establish the relevant local pathway.
