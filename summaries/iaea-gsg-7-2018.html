2. Authority and jurisdiction

  • International guidance for implementing occupational protection requirements in GSR Part 3. It does not directly impose duties on THHS.
  • The Queensland Radiation Safety Act and Regulation define the local statutory RSO role and worker-monitoring duties. The guide's radiation protection officer (RPO) is a functional comparison, not a claim of identical certification or powers.

3. Key takeaways

  • Management owns the radiation protection programme and supplies resources and qualified advice.
  • An RPO is appointed where required by the regulatory body to oversee application of requirements and compliance (para 3.66).
  • Monitoring is based on expected exposure and workplace conditions; individual dosimetry is used where appropriate (paras 3.100–3.118).
  • Investigation levels prompt review of unexpected results or trends; they are distinct from dose limits and dose constraints (paras 3.122–3.127).
  • Individual exposure records need to be complete, retrievable and protected against loss (paras 3.132–3.140).

4. Summary

4.1. Programme design and responsibilities

  • The occupational protection programme identifies roles, workplace conditions and measures proportionate to risk.

4.1.1. Radiation protection officer and qualified experts (paras 3.60–3.68)

  • Management secures qualified expertise and appoints an RPO when required by the regulator. Technical advice and day-to-day oversight support, but do not replace, management responsibility.

4.1.2. Monitoring and assessment (paras 3.96–3.121)

  • Management designs workplace and individual monitoring using prior radiological assessment, the work performed and regulatory requirements.

4.1.3. Investigation levels (paras 3.122–3.127)

  • Define a trigger and investigation process in advance; review the result and circumstances, then consider corrective action and lessons.

4.1.4. Exposure records (paras 3.132–3.140)

  • Maintain accurate, intelligible and retrievable individual dose records with appropriate protection against loss.

5. Practical relationship to the THHS RSPP

  • Helps trace the local RSO, personal-monitoring, unusual-dose investigation and exposure-record controls to international occupational-protection guidance.
  • Use the Queensland Act, Regulation and approved RSPP for operative local duties and thresholds.
