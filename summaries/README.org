* Purpose
This directory contains concise, source-faithful notes for the primary documents in the project corpus. Most source PDFs are retained locally; the newer IAEA transport, security and occupational-protection PDFs are linked at their official IAEA URLs. Each note distinguishes the document's authority, jurisdiction and practical relationship to the approved THHS Radiation Therapy RSPP.

The summaries are study aids. For an operative requirement, consult the current source document, applicable Queensland law, statutory standards, licence or approval conditions, and controlled THHS documents.

* Prioritised source and summary queue
1. /Queensland Standard for ionising radiation apparatus—medical imaging (2021)/ — statutory standard relevant to in-scope imaging apparatus; official download remains pending.
2. Relevant current /UNSCEAR/ evaluations — scientific evidence layer for radiation effects and risk; select reports by the RSPP practice being traced rather than treating UNSCEAR as a regulator.
3. Any applicable THHS possession-licence, use-authorisation or approval conditions suitable for the project — these may create enforceable site-specific links but require access and review before inclusion.
4. /RPS 14.1 — Safety Guide for Radiation Protection in Diagnostic and Interventional Radiology/ — low priority because its stated scope excludes radiotherapy, including treatment planning with dedicated equipment; retain only if a genuinely separate diagnostic/interventional practice enters scope.
5. /RPS 14.2 — Safety Guide for Radiation Protection in Nuclear Medicine/ — supplementary for a defined nuclear-medicine interface, such as a radiopharmaceutical patient attending radiotherapy; its scope excludes external-beam and sealed-source radiotherapy itself.

* Authority reading order
The corpus is not one strict chain. A useful reading order is:

scientific evidence → international recommendations/fundamentals → international requirements/guidance → Australian fundamentals/codes/guidance → Queensland Act/Regulation/statutory standards → licence and approval conditions → approved RSPP → departmental procedures → implementation records.

Only the relevant Queensland instruments, licence/approval conditions and approved local requirements are directly enforceable at THHS. Other documents can be authoritative influences, nationally adoptable codes, technical guidance or evidence without themselves being Queensland law.
